02

The arguments against us

Our own survey of adjacent work records these, and we publish them. A comparison page that carries its own counter-evidence is unusual enough to be the difference.

A neighboring project declined this whole approach.

A project solving a neighboring problem looked at the same standards stack and chose not to adopt it. Their reasons are recorded in our survey, and they are not paraphrased away.

The opposite mechanism has the enterprise adoption.

TrustGraph ships extraction rather than declaration, and it is the one with named enterprise deployments. Declaration asks authors for work that extraction does not.

The closest analog is in another language.

Vale is the nearest observed analog to this engine, and its lesson cuts both ways: the command-line checker is not the product. The place a person cannot easily self-host is.

The survey licenses no efficacy conclusion at all.

Adjacent projects converge on this substrate, and almost none of them measured it. Convergence is a shared prior, not evidence. Every efficacy claim in this repository is marked unmeasured until the measurement layer, milestone M7, runs.

The full record is this project's evaluation of adjacent work. It is not linked here, because the repository is not public yet. The self-assessment states what a run of this engine measures instead.
