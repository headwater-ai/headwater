02

The arguments against Headwater

Our own survey of adjacent work found these reasons to doubt the approach.

A neighboring project declined the standards stack.

A project that solves a neighboring problem looked at the same standards stack and chose not to adopt it. The survey records its reasons.

The opposite mechanism has the enterprise adoption.

TrustGraph extracts where Headwater declares, and TrustGraph is the one with named enterprise deployments. Declaration asks authors for work that extraction does not.

The closest analog does not sell its checker.

Vale is the nearest analog to this engine, and its command-line checker is not the product. The product is the hosted part that a person cannot easily run alone.

The survey does not show that the approach works.

Adjacent projects chose a similar foundation, and almost none of them measured the result. Their agreement is a shared assumption. This repository has measured only itself, and the self-assessment says what that found. It has measured nothing against these projects.

The full survey is adjacent-work.md in the repository. The self-assessment states what this engine measures about itself. To judge it on your own files, run the tutorial.

The source of this project is github.com/headwater-ai/headwater, under Apache-2.0. The engine, the taxonomy library and the corpus these pages describe are all in it.
