# A naming survey

## The question

Whether "attempt" or "dispatch" is the term that a reader of the model recognizes without a definition.

## The instrument

Five neighboring systems were read for the term each one uses, and eight operators were asked to define both terms cold.

## What it found

Four of five systems use "attempt". Seven of eight operators defined it correctly, against three of eight for "dispatch".
