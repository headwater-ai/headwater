# Two meta-schema surfaces have a required declaration and no stated form

## Context

A facet declares `volatility`, and no value set states what it may hold. The [ascertainability canon](../spec/02-taxonomy-model.md#facet-acceptance-tests) requires guidance on every enum value, and no syntax carries it. A third surface is smaller and it belongs beside them. [Spec 7](../spec/07-distribution-and-federation.md#publishing) declares bundles in the package manifest, and nothing fixes the shape of the file that holds one. The first library entry guessed all three, and it recorded each guess.

## Obligation

The meta-schema measures the class rather than reporting three of it. `engine/crates/meta/meta-schema.yml` marks ten positions with the word `gap`, where a declaration is required and no form is stated. Guidance is one of them. Three more sit on a regime or a relation. They are the constructions that a voice regime forbids, the profile names that `controlled` admits, and the form of `invalid_when`. Three more are the glob constructs of a shelf path, the parts of an identifier pattern, and the allocation strategies. The last three sit on `obligations` and `controls`. They are the defect class that `prevents` names, the trigger, and the three members of the promotion record.

The defect class is the widest of them. [Spec 4](../spec/04-assurance-model.md) derives that taxonomy from real documentation problems and walks nine classes in prose, and no declaration in the language holds it.

## Discharge

Two positions left this count when the register landed. The mechanism grammar now has one, because the engine reads `check:` and `phase:` and treats every other prefix as a mechanism outside it. The posture left because it became two members, each with a closed set.

One more left it when `headwater generate` landed. Closing the projection kinds turned one question into two. Eight kinds are declarable, and the engine defines two more at a path it fixes. [Spec 6](../spec/06-engine-architecture.md#projections) now states that split, and the meta-schema carries the declarable eight as an enum.
