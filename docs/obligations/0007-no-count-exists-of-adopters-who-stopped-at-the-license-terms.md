# No count exists of adopters who stopped at the license terms

## Context

[Q11](../spec/09-decisions.md#q11--license-and-distribution-posture) settles Apache-2.0 for the engine, the library, the base package and the bundles. Permissive terms should remove a step before a trial.

## Obligation

The instrument is a count of adopters who report the terms as the reason that they stopped.

## Discharge

That count needs a public channel, which does not exist yet. Nothing else can supply it, because a private trial leaves no record of the reason anybody declined one.
