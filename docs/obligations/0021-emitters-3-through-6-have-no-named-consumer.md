# Emitters 3 through 6 have no named consumer

## Context

[Q13](../spec/09-decisions.md#q13--linkml-and-shacl-as-substrate) stages six emitters and rules that they never chain. Emitters 3 through 6 have no named consumer.

## Obligation

The corpus owes a named consumer for each of the four, or the four are never written.

## Discharge

None exists, and that is the trigger rather than an oversight. If none appears, four emitters are never written and nothing upstream changes.

This item is data or a deferred component, so the first real adopter is the evidence rather than a further argument.
