# Whether an adjudication is ever partial

## Context

[Q18](../spec/09-decisions.md#q18--recording-adjudicated-disagreements) rules how an adjudicated disagreement is recorded.

## Obligation

The corpus owes a ruling on whether an adjudication is ever partial, with one document governing one axis and another governing a second.

## Discharge

Today that is two edges, or one document whose prose carries the split. No corpus shows the need.
