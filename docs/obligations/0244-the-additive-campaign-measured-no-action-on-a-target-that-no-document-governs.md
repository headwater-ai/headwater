# The additive campaign measured no action on a target that no document governs

## Context

[HW-DR-0112](../decisions/0112-the-additive-campaign-measures-eleven-actions-by-five-layers-in-seven-scenarios.md#the-circumstance-axes) names three circumstance axes that the additive campaign defers and records as debt. Tasks outside anything a document governs is the second. The campaign said to record them after its last paid session, and phase B on #1881 was that session. In [the evaluation of phase B](../evaluations/what-the-additive-campaign-measured-for-the-eleven-actions-headwater-supports.md), row 11, the ungoverned control, measures harm on one such task. No other row does.

## Obligation

The corpus owes a measurement of how the layers change an action whose target no document governs. Rows 1 to 10 each point at a governed target, so each one measures the layers where a document applies. The ungoverned control row measures only that the layers do no harm, and not one of the other actions on an ungoverned target.

## Discharge

This record discharges when a run measures at least one of rows 1 to 10 on targets that no document governs. The run must compare it with the same row on governed targets, and state both rates and the difference, with its interval.
