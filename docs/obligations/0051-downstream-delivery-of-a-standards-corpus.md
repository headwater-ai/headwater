# Downstream delivery of a standards corpus

## Context

[Spec 7](../spec/07-distribution-and-federation.md#what-is-shared-and-what-is-not) shares a schema, doctrine prose and the publisher's obligations, and it shares no corpus content. So a publisher whose method includes standards documents reaches a consumer with a conformance rule, and never with a document. The same specification rules that a reference runs upward, and no resolver in [spec 2](../spec/02-taxonomy-model.md#behavior-at-the-limits) carries content down to meet it.

## Obligation

The parts of a third arrangement exist already: an export profile, a committed pin at the consumer, and one anchor kind over that pin. Each edge into a changed item then takes a drift finding, exactly as [Q19](../spec/09-decisions.md#q19--inbound-integration-an-external-system-of-record) rules for a requirements snapshot. No section composes those parts downward, and no entry in the register rules on the direction.

## Discharge

Either one section states that arrangement, or corpus content never travels downward and a shared standard is always a package rule. [Q9](../spec/09-decisions.md#q9--multi-repository-corpora) holds the tier that would carry it, and it has no instance.
