# Two of the four warrant values have no instance in this corpus

## Context

Every authored document here is `accepted`, because a human merged each one. `regenerated` has an instance now. [`docs/spec/README.md`](../spec/README.md) is the generated index of the specification shelf. The census reads that warrant off the marker rather than out of a declaration, and that file carries the marker on its first line.

## Obligation

Nothing is `transcribed`, because no external system of record feeds this repository. Nothing is `asserted`, because the corpus holds no content that a human declined to accept. Four engine rules turn on the value, and this corpus exercises two of them.

## Discharge

Two of the four branches run over corpus content, and not over the census fixture tree alone. The other two wait on an importer and on a synthesized tier, and neither exists here.
