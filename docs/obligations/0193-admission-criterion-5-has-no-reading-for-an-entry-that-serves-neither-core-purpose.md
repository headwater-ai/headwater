# Admission criterion 5 has no reading for an entry that serves neither core purpose

## Context

[Criterion 5](../taxonomies/README.md#admission-criteria) asks an entry to name which of its facets carry the core roles, and which of its kinds serve `rationale` and `behavior`. It supplies an escape for one shape only, which is a facet-only overlay that composes onto a base.

The [`brd-prd` entry](../taxonomies/brd-prd/doctrine.md#findings) is not that shape. It declares two kinds, a purpose, a voice regime, a facet, a relation, a shelf and two identifier schemes. It serves a third reader intent instead of either core purpose. Its first finding at [line 163](https://github.com/headwater-ai/headwater/blob/5fb9518/docs/taxonomies/brd-prd/doctrine.md#L163) holds the argument, and the entry took a reading rather than a refusal.

**The reading is not new, and that is what makes it worth recording.** The [`decision-record` entry](../taxonomies/decision-record/doctrine.md#the-core-declared) reached the same position first and read the criterion the same way, without naming the gap. Two entries have therefore read criterion 5 this way, independently.

## Obligation

The owner owes a wording of criterion 5 that either admits an entry with a third reader intent or refuses one. The criterion is the gate for every later entry. Two of the five admitted entries passed it on a reading that the text does not carry.

[#520](https://github.com/headwater-ai/headwater/issues/520) is the ruling, and it covers criteria 2 and 5 together. The criterion-2 half is [HW-OBL-0186](0186-a-facet-only-entry-has-no-kind-and-the-entry-anatomy-asks-for-two-things-that-presume-one.md). This record is the criterion-5 half, and it states the gap where a reader of this register meets it.

## Discharge

This record discharges when criterion 5 states what it asks of an entry that serves neither core purpose, with the reason. A ruling that the present wording is correct discharges it as fully as a ruling that changes the wording. Either one says which reading the two entries should take.
