The shipped skill bodies call `headwater`, and `headwater` is not on the `PATH` of a session, so without that section a session cannot run the command that a skill names.

The canary of 8 sessions was read before `canary-passed` was written. Every flagged record stated that the session stayed in its workspace, that no fetch request was refused, and that no host file was readable at the outside paths it named. It did not launch the batch again. The `tree` digest in the identity above is the digest the assembly recorded.
