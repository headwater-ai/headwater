The trial session this run started with (`cold-current`, below) was recorded before the fix and its transcript was reassembled from the same raw log afterward at no further cost; the other seven were driven after the fix landed.

The eight sessions spent 58, 49, 23, 22, 33, 69, 64 and 72 cents against a declared 25 cents each, for a total of $3.90 against the $20.00 ceiling — higher than every prior run, consistent with a larger model doing more reading per session.
