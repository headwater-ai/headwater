The diagnosis behind round 2 comes from the confirmation of round 1. All 14 sessions that used no skill, and 14 of the 18 failures, were on the recorder-contract probe. On that probe, each session searched for the named script before it loaded any skill.

The driver halted six times at 2 of 25 sessions, during the canary, when the harness failed in the way that a usage limit stops a session. It continued in the same try root at the same pin, and it did not launch the batch again. The `tree` digest in the identity above is the digest the assembly recorded.
