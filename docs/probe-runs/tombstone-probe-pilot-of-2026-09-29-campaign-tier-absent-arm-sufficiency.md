**This is a pilot of one probe and not the campaign.** `tools/probe/campaign.sh` recorded it on 2026-09-29 (UTC) for the first step of [#1384](https://github.com/headwater-ai/headwater/issues/1384). It ran the probe `HW-PROBE-a-counted-tombstone-separates-a-withheld-answer-from-an-absent-answer` alone, at 10 repetitions per arm, after the changes to the recorder, the grader, the read set and the seal had merged (#1394, #1396, #1414 and #1413). The owner ruled on #1384 on 2026-09-30 (AEST) that this pilot spends no more than $10.00, at 10 sessions per arm. No comparison pools it with the campaign of 2026-09-28, because the seal, the turn cap and the harness moved between the two.

**One batch, one tree.** Every session ran in a fresh copy of one `git archive` of commit `59d3bc1a`, an empty pin commit on `16eecca0`, with the engine that commit builds. The sessions ran in one order that seed 980 shuffled, two at a time, on `claude-sonnet-5` with a cap of 80 turns, and the cap stopped 0 of the 10 sessions of this arm. Each workspace lost the instrument, every record under `docs/` that names the probe, and every file outside `docs/` that names it and is not a declared fold. Each absent workspace also lost the ablation of the campaign tier. The `seed` in the identity above is the plan's seed, and the shuffle seed is not a member of the identity.

**What the seal cannot find.** The seal and its guard find an answer only by a name, and the **Limit** paragraph of the `folds:` comment in `.headwater/probe.yml` states that bound.
