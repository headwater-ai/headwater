# The live record resting on a draft

The decisive case for the initial reading. `mentions` is `association`, and
nothing marks it lifecycle-sensitive. A draft has no record to cite: its
declared guidance is that nothing may rely on it, so a live document that
points at one through any relation is resting on a document nobody promoted.
