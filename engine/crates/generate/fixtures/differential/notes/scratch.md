# A note that states a facet that its own kind forbids

The `note` kind forbids `audience`, and this document states it. No check in the registry reports that, so the engine finds nothing here. A schema that carried the prohibition as `not` reports an error, and the two claimed families then stop describing what the artifact rejects.
